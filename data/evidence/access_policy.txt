ACCESS CONTROL POLICY — FormFlow Inc.
Effective Date: January 2026 | Owner: IT Security Team

1. PURPOSE
This policy governs logical and physical access to FormFlow's information systems,
including production infrastructure, customer data stores, and internal tooling.

2. ACCESS PROVISIONING
All employee access is provisioned via Okta SSO, following the principle of least
privilege. Access requests require manager approval and are logged in the IT
service desk ticketing system. Role-based access control (RBAC) is enforced for
all production systems, with distinct roles for Engineering, Support, and Admin.

3. ACCESS REVIEW
Access entitlements are reviewed quarterly by system owners. Any accounts inactive
for more than 90 days are automatically disabled and flagged for manager review.

4. TERMINATION
Upon employee termination, IT Security revokes all system access within 4 business
hours of notification from HR, per the offboarding checklist.

5. PHYSICAL ACCESS
Office access is controlled via badge entry system; visitor logs are maintained
and reviewed monthly by the Office Operations team.
